# The World in One Day — 21 November 2026

[![make.fun · news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261121.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121)

Sum up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image. An AI judge
that can see images looks at two at a time and picks the one that best sums up
the day's world news.

The image covers eight stories, verified against Reuters, AP, CNN and NYT
reporting of 21 November 2026:

- **Lead — COP31 close to a climate deal:** Antalya talks run past deadline;
  fossil fuels and a $120bn adaptation fund still deadlocked.
- **Gulf crisis — Iran strikes Bahrain:** Tehran says its Guards hit two US
  bases; Bahrain reports an industrial-site fire, no injuries.
- **Mexico — ‘El Chapo Isidro’ captured:** FBI most-wanted cartel boss Fausto
  Isidro Meza Flores seized in Sinaloa; at least 11 die as violence erupts.
- **Ukraine — Missile hits Kryvyi Rih:** a Russian strike on Zelenskyy's
  hometown kills a boy, 7, and a paramedic; 28 in hospital; missiles stray
  into Poland.
- **Baltic Sea — Russian ship ablaze:** drones hit the sanctioned cargo ship
  Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland; the crew evacuates.
- **Markets:** Nasdaq's worst week since March 2020 as the AI sell-off turns
  into a correction.
- **Hope:** mosquitoes cut dengue in Brazilian children by about 69%.
- **Summit:** Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen; Zelenskyy calls Putin a
  "killer".

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes /output/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB)
```

`entry.png` was generated offline by `build_image.py` (Pillow, DejaVu fonts)
and is bundled here; `main.py` copies it deterministically with the standard
library only.
